# Monday, October 12th, 2026

### 23:46 UTC-04

We are currently through elections in Brazil right now. More exactly, between the
first and second rounds. I remember that I always liked it when I was younger, watching
the political ads and always seeing the polls on the television. But, since I started
voting and really analyzing what happens during a campaign, I started hating it.
It seems to me, now, that the idea of both sides is to deceive better than the opposition.
When everything is based on lies, how can we ever chose a candidate that we believe
would be better for our lives? In the end, we end up always chosing the one we hate
the least, instead the one we would like the most.
