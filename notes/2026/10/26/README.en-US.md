### 02:37 UTC-04

Sadness. I just cannot believe that what was statistically impossible actually happened.
I just cannot believe that Lula was, once again, for the 4th time, elected president.
I can only recall what the last 4 years have been and... I'm completely lost for
words. I followed every poll, every projection, every prediction: absolutely no
one imagined that this result was possible. Everyone, absolutely everyone was wrong.
It makes me sad because I've been following it closely, seeing how the numbers were
changing through the days, and I was absolutely confident that we would finally
get rid of this president, that for me, holds Brazil back for decades now. I do
understand that the opposite candidate was not perfect, far from it, but what is
democracy if not power exchange? How come we have the same president ever since
my brothers were born? They are 24 already! I'm not desperate, I believe my life
will just stay the same... But I love my country, and I do not believe we, as a
whole, have made the best choice.
